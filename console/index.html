Results

The first score focuses on reach: views compared with similar organic posts at the same age. Shares and completed viewing are supporting signals. Sample support describes the amount of data, not proof that a draft caused the outcome. Paid exposure and changes in audience or timing can distort a comparison.

Export learning examples

Downloads JSONL with the brief, preferred draft and other draft. A separate comparison audit includes post links, observations and declared origins. Only eligible learning experiments enter the export; skipped comparisons and their reasons appear below. Exporting does not start training.
