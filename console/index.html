Experiments

Compare drafts against real TikTok and Instagram results. Record publication after a post is live; this page does not publish posts or train a model. Keep the same platform, account, format and posting context within an experiment. Automatic checks need a connected analytics account and its account ID. You can also enter counts manually.

For unattended TikTok checks, connect the refresh token and app credentials as well as the access token. An access-token-only connection needs manual renewal after expiry.

Results

The first score focuses on reach: views compared with similar organic posts at the same age. Shares and completed viewing are supporting signals. Sample support describes the amount of data, not proof that a draft caused the outcome. Paid exposure and changes in audience or timing can distort a comparison.

Prepares download links for JSONL with the brief, preferred draft and other draft, and a separate comparison audit with post links, observations and declared origins. Only eligible learning experiments enter the export; skipped comparisons and their reasons appear below. Exporting does not start training.
